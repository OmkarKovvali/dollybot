## Docking Station Installation Instructions

!!! note About Parameters
For parameters regarding the docking station, GNSS, 4G, and other modules, please refer to [About A2](https://support.unitree.com/home/en/A2_SDK_Development_Guide/about_a2).
!!!

### Assembling the Docking Station

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/0bec0d899ecf4bdeac1d9cc76c483043_1389x554.png)

Place the antenna box in the center at the rear of the docking station, insert screws through the antenna box, and tighten them onto the docking station. Affix the GNSS module to the right side of the antenna box.

!!! warning GNSS and Antenna Box Vertical Signal Interference
The GNSS module and the antenna box will cause vertical signal interference. Therefore, the GNSS module must be placed next to the antenna box and cannot be affixed on top of it.
!!!

### Installing the Docking Station to the A2

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/72f34a93906140ae9ad2afbc1538a351_1489x1217.png)

<center> Step 1: Insert the M6 hex nut provided with the shipment into the rear slide rail, and tighten the docking station using the M6x14 screws. </center>

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/127ca3bbebf34731b6efe89d76e9fc33_1484x1197.png)

<center> Step 2: Open both side covers and the top cable cover. </center>

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/33e2bd9478d147119ef6ac6990d44f71_1433x1184.png)

<center> Step 3: Locate the aviation plug interface (PC1 Ethernet) on the left side of the A2 body and connect it to the first aviation port on the left side of the docking station as shown in the diagram. </center>

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/5b16795cadf0454580baaa408825daed_1520x1181.png)

<center> Step 4: Locate the aviation plug interface (58V Output) on the right side of the A2 body and connect it to the first aviation port on the right side of the docking station as shown in the diagram. </center>

![](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2026/6/22/b004de512fce4f7fb1e26de942ee01df_1364x1194.png)

<center> Step 5: Replace both side covers and tighten them. </center>


!!! note Using the Docking Station

You can refer to the [Docking Station User Manual](https://support.unitree.com/home/en/A2_SDK_Development_Guide/about_a2_nx) for instructions on how to use and develop with the docking station.

!!!
