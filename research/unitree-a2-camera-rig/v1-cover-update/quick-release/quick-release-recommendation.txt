RECOMMENDED: FALCAM F38 MULTI-HOLE BASE & PLATE KIT F38B3404
Manufacturer-listed assembled product envelope 53.4 x 38 x 20.8 mm; mass 51.7 g; 6061 aluminum. Select the Base & Plate Kit, not the plate-only option. The 3364 base alone is 53.4 x 38 x 13.5 mm / 36 g; matching non-slip camera plate is 3301. Push in to engage; press to release after disabling the secondary lock. Sliding/pushing the secondary control to the locked position blocks release-button depression. Manufacturer says align camera lens with the F38 button. Keep that control accessible and preserve its full sideways lock travel and push stroke. The base accepts F38-spec plates only: its Arca statement does not establish acceptance of arbitrary Arca camera plates. Price observed on Ulanzi kit page: USD36.99, informational only.
