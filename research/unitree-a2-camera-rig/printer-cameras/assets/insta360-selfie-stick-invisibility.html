Selfie Sticks: How to Make Different Types of Selfie Sticks Invisible

Selfie Sticks: How to Make Different Types of Selfie Sticks Invisible

Applicable Models: 85cm Invisible Selfie Stick, 114cm Invisible Selfie Stick, Action Invisible Selfie Stick, Bullet Time Accessories, Bullet Time Selfie Stick 2.0, Enhanced Extended Edition Selfie Stick, Mini 2-in-1 Tripod, Mini 2-in-1 Tripod 2.0, Foldable 2-in-1 Selfie Stick (1/4" Mount), and Multi Mount

Handheld selfie sticks

Suitable for 360 camera + 360 lens
  • Complete invisibility cannot be achieved when using an Extended Edition Selfie Stick.
  • Notes for invisibility with a Power Selfie Stick:
    • When installing the Power Selfie Stick, keep the camera and the selfie stick aligned in a straight line to allow the camera's invisibility algorithm to process the selfie stick image. If there is a certain tilt angle between the camera and the selfie stick, invisibility of the selfie stick will not be possible.
    • When the camera is connected to the selfie stick via a charging cable, the USB port of the charging cable may appear in the frame, preventing complete invisibility.
  • Notes for invisibility with other selfie sticks:
    • When installing the Power Selfie Stick, keep the camera and the selfie stick aligned in a straight line to allow the camera's invisibility algorithm to process the selfie stick image.
    • Please keep the selfie stick stable during shooting. Any slight movement may affect the invisibility effect.
    • When using X5, X4, X4 Air, or X3 with the Invisible Dive Case underwater, it's possible to make the selfie stick invisible; when X4 Air, X2, or ONE X are used with the Dive Case, the selfie stick can be invisible, but the bottom of the Dive Case cannot be invisible.

For a more detailed tutorial on selfie stick invisibility, please refer to: Tutorial on Selfie Stick Invisibility

Applicable to Ace series cameras
  • Complete invisibility cannot be achieved when using an Extended Edition Selfie Stick.
  • Notes for invisibility with a Power Selfie Stick:
    • When installing the Power Selfie Stick, keep the camera and the selfie stick aligned in a straight line to allow the camera's invisibility algorithm to process the selfie stick image.
    • When the camera is connected to the selfie stick via a charging cable, the USB port of the charging cable may appear in the frame, preventing complete invisibility.
  • For other selfie sticks, selfie stick removal during post editing is supported:

    Open the Insta360 app > tap "Edit" > select "Shot Lab" > find "AI Selfie Stick Eraser" > tap "Use This Theme" to make the selfie stick invisible.

For a more detailed tutorial on selfie stick removal, please refer to: Tutorial on Selfie Stick Removal

A GO series camera, 360 camera + wide-angle lens

If you are using a GO Series camera or a wide-angle lens (e.g., X3's Single-Lens Mode), the Invisible Selfie Stick is currently not supported. You can adjust the shooting angle to avoid the selfie stick appearing in the frame.

Mini 2-in-1 Tripod 2.0

If you are using a 360 camera, when connecting the camera to the selfie stick, rotate the knob to secure the camera, ensuring the thumb screw is parallel to the bottom of the camera, and the camera is parallel to the selfie stick — this will make the selfie stick invisible during shooting.
