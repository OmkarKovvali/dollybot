1.2 Top Tips

Can the selfie sticks be used in seawater?

The selfie stick can be used underwater, but after use, please make sure to extend all sections, rinse thoroughly with clean water, and ensure they are dry before storing to prevent residual stains from damaging the stick.

Note: the Power Selfie Stick and the Extended Edition Selfie Stick (either enhanced or original version) are not suitable for use underwater or in seawater.

How to make a selfie stick invisible?

We've got a full guide on how to use the Invisible Selfie Stick here, but some key things to remember:

  • 360º only: In case we weren't clear, the selfie stick's ability to disappear is only possible with 360-degree footage. If you're using this with one of our Ace series cameras, you can use the AI Selfie Stick Eraser in the app for a similar effect, but it won't be anywhere like the real thing.

  • Get the mounting right: Try to keep the two camera lenses parallel with the stick—right down the middle.

  • Choose the right length: For vlogs where you're the main subject, we recommend a distance of about 50–70 centimeters. If you're doing video-game-style third-person shots, extend the stick to its maximum length. And if you want to go aerial, go for our 3-meter-long option.

  • Positioning: Due to the way fisheye lenses can exaggerate distance, it's best to put the camera a bit closer to your subject than you might with a standard camera. Don't worry, you can get rid of the fisheye look in the edit and revert to a natural, undistorted perspective.

  • Get creative: You'll find a variety of unique effects that take full advantage of the Invisible Selfie Stick in the Shot Lab section of the Insta360 app. Shot Lab uses a smart AI-powered editing algorithm to analyze your shots and automatically turn them into an awesome final edit, and you don't even need to plan ahead.
