BAMBU LAB H2S
Official build volume: 340 W x 320 D x 340 H mm. Official spec-sheet PDF downloaded and page 1 visually inspected. Max nozzle 350 C; heated chamber max 65 C; shipped nozzle listed as 0.4 mm hardened steel. Nominal build volume is not a promise that a part filling it will slice with a brim/support/prime tower. For concept planning each concept module is targeted at no more than 280 x 280 mm, comfortably below the nominal XY envelope. This is a provisional print-envelope target, not a fixed part dimension. Do not use H2D/H2C limits for this user's H2S.

SONY ZV-E1
Official envelope (body): 121.0 W x 71.9 H x 54.3 D mm. Approximate mass 483 g with battery and SD card, before lens/cage/accessories. Official help guide identifies bottom 1/4-20 UNC tripod socket and requires screw shorter than 5.5 mm entering the camera. For an adapter, control screw projection beyond its camera-contact surface rather than confusing total bolt length with engagement. Do not turn 5.5 mm into a target: it is a strict upper limit, with engagement and fit to be checked physically. The bottom illustration is a part-identification drawing, not dimensioned manufacturing CAD. Preserve access to the battery door, side connectors and folding monitor, and room for the lens zoom envelope. No authoritative public STEP/CAD model or dimensioned tripod-socket XY datum drawing was located in this search.

DESIGN INPUTS TO CARRY FORWARD (ENGINEERING JUDGMENT, NOT MANUFACTURER CERTIFICATION)
Use a low Sony mount on a common load-spreading robot deck; use a separately braced pole socket/paired clamp that transfers bending over two support heights. A long stick held only by one small axial screw is not the intended load path. Existing metal quick-release and camera screws are preferable interfaces for a printed adapter. Add a mechanical anti-rotation feature and captive hardware; use broad grip contact without crushing a telescoping section. Keep the pole near robot centerline as space allows. A bracket must not interfere with battery removal, lidar/camera sight lines, cooling, body handles or moving limbs; A2 source research owns those exact keep-outs.
Use the Sony and X6 on their own batteries for initial concept. External power requires a separate verified A2 power-interface design; camera USB cannot be connected to an unspecified robot supply.
No combined payload mass is final: 483 g body + 353 g confirmed lens + 196 g X6 + provisional 118 g stick = 1,150 g before cage, deck, mounting hardware and any battery changes. Re-weigh the actual rig; the old 118 g stick value may not apply. A 200 g camera at 1.14 m contributes approximately 2.24 N m under a transverse 1 g load, before stick weight and dynamic amplification; vertical self-weight alone is not this transverse case. Height/acceleration and pole base stiffness therefore matter even if overall payload is far below A2's headline payload.
