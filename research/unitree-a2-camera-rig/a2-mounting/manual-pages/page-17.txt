You can then use the companion remote controller to operate it. The specific steps
are as follows:

Briefly press the M function button; the robotic dog will enter Companion Mode and emit a voice prompt.

d) If the robotic dog emits a voice prompt indicating the distance is too far, it means the distance between
the remote controller and the robotic dog exceeds 2.4 meters, exceeding the companion range. Move closer
and repeat Step 3.

Step 3: Exit Companion Mode

a) Press the P button on the companion remote controller or move the joystick. The robotic dog will exit
Companion Mode, enter Joystick Mode, and emit a voice prompt.

b) Place the companion remote controller horizontally, or tilt it at an angle greater than 40° compared to
its activated state. The robotic dog will exit Companion Mode, enter Joystick Mode, and emit a voice prompt.

c) Press any button on a two-handed remote controller or move the joystick. The robotic dog will exit
Companion Mode, enter Joystick Mode, and emit a voice prompt.

d) Press and hold the power button on the companion remote controller for 2 seconds to turn it off, exiting

Companion Mode. Restarting the remote controller will automatically re-enter Companion Mode.
