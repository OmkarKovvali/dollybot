Unitree A2 Stellar Explorer is an agile, lightweight, and long-endurance industrial-grade quadruped robot.

6061-T6 aluminum alloy, with key components utilizing 7075-T6, enabling it to withstand impact from falls.

precise perception. Combined with intelligent algorithms, it easily handles complex scenarios such as logistics,
inspection, and rescue operations. The standard configuration includes an 8-core high-performance CPU (for

module available. With its medium-sized stature delivering powerful industrial-grade performance, it is an

ideal choice for operations in extreme environments.
