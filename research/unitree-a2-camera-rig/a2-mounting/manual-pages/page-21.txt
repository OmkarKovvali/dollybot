3) How to Shut Down the Robot When the Remote Control Module Fails

When the remote control module fails (e.g., due to depleted battery), making it impossible to use the
remote control to put the robot into a lying standby state, you can only force a shutdown by pressing the battery
power button.

Forced Shutdown: Maintain a safe distance of at least 2 meters between the robot and obstacles, complex
terrain, crowds, water surfaces, etc. Lift the robot's head and tail, briefly press the power switch once, then
press and hold the power switch for more than 3 seconds to turn off the power. After the robot powers off,
slowly place it on the ground.

4) Robot Prone to Falling, Unable to Stand After Startup

Incorrect startup posture may cause errors in the robot's motor angles. Use the correct startup posture and
restart the robot. If restarting the robot does not resolve the issue, please contact Unitree official technical
support.

5) How to Achieve Long-Term Standby

If long-term standby is required, control the robot into a lying posture (press L2+B to enter damping
mode) to prevent the robot from automatically shutting down due to low battery and falling, which could cause

damage!

A @ Attention! When lifting the robot, do not place your hands on the joints, such as the hip joint, to avoid
pinching. If the robot is forced to shut down while standing without protection, it will fall flat on the ground

after losing power.

Precautions

1) Ensure the robot is in a prone position before shutting it down. Otherwise, when the robot powers off,
it will fall heavily to the ground, which may cause damage to the body and pose safety hazards!

2) When standing, if the ground friction is insufficient or the robot's feet do not have reliable support, do
not violently adjust the robot's posture (including pitch, roll, yaw, or body height adjustments), as this may
cause the robot to lose balance and fall.

3) Operate the robot to walk on flat, stable terrain. When walking on surfaces with low friction, avoid
operating the robot violently, as this may cause the feet to slip, leading to instability and falls.
