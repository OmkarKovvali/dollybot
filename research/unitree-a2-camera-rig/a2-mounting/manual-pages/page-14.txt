5) Please do not run the robot in the WiFi signal interference environment. WiFi signal interference is
usually caused by co-channel interference. In case of interference, be sure to turn off some or all WiFi signal
sources of other wireless devices before using the remote control to operate the robot.

6) Due to varying levels of proficiency among actual operators, for the sake of reliability and safety,
please use the robot in an open, unobstructed, and flat ground environment. When operating the robot, be
mindful to avoid steps higher than 30cm, slopes steeper than 45°, and obstacles that may cause the robot to
fall. When the robot is walking on complex ground or terrain with some unevenness and slopes, the operator
should reduce the robot's walking speed and operate with caution to prevent the robot from tripping over
obstacles.

7) Legged robots have certain requirements for the walking surface. Do not use the robot on surfaces with
very low friction, such as ice. Do not use the robot on soft surfaces, such as thick foam flooring. If used on
relatively smooth surfaces, such as glass, ceramic tiles, etc., carefully and gently maneuver the robot, avoid

sudden movements, and reduce the robot's walking speed to prevent the robot's feet from slipping and causing
a fall.
