1) We recommend you to use a 5V/1A USB charger which meets FCC/CE standard.

2) The power indicator light will flash at 1Hz (1 second/time) in charging status and indicate the current
power level.

3) When the power indicator light is all off, it means the battery pack is full, please remove the charger
to finish charging.
