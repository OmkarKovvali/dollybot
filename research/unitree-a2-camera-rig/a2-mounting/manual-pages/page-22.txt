5) Regarding Steps and Uneven Terrain: Due to varying skill levels among operators, for reliability
and safety, it is currently not recommended for users to navigate steps exceeding 30cm in height, as improper
operation may cause the robot to trip. When encountering uneven ground, operators should proceed with
caution and reduce the robot's movement speed.

6) Regarding Slope Climbing: The rated forward climbing angle is less than or equal to 45°. When
climbing at larger angles (approximately equal to or greater than 45°), the robot's body may drift sideways.
Turning directly on steep slopes may cause the robot to lose balance. When climbing slopes, reduce walking
speed and ensure proper operator control.

7) Regarding Speed: Under stable operation on flat ground, the speed ranges from approximately 0 to
3.7 m/s, with a maximum of about 5 m/s.

8) Regarding Foot Components: The robot's foot components are consumables. We provide spare foot
components with the product. Wear can be significant, especially when operating on rough surfaces. If
noticeable wear or damage to the foot pads is observed, or if the impact noise during walking increases
significantly, replace the foot components promptly to prevent abnormal robot movement or damage due to
worn foot components.

9) Regarding IP56 Protection Level: Before use, ensure the battery interface, battery compartment
interface, battery surface, and battery compartment surface are dry and free of moisture before inserting the
battery into the robot.

10) Do not touch the robot while it is in motion! Be cautious of pinching at the joints, such as the

knee joints.
