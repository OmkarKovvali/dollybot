Daily Maintenance

Whole Machine Clean

1) Clean

When the A2 is used in harsh environments (rainy, dusty, wet), please clean the surface of the robot body
in time.

Before cleaning the robot body, please turn off the power, use a clean, soft cloth to wipe the body,
especially pay attention to whether the camera is wiped clean. It is strictly forbidden to use metal brushes,
sandpaper, etc. to clean to avoid scratching the surface of the parts. After cleaning, dry the body with a soft
cloth. After drying, use a fan or air gun to dry the accumulated water on the surface of the robot and the joint

gap to avoid leaving water marks.

A @ Be careful! The whole A2 robot (including battery pack) has IP56 waterproof. Do not remove the battery

pack when cleaning!

2) Storage
The A2 should be stored in a dry and cool room and away from the sun and rain to avoid the corrosion of

parts and shorten the service life span. Take out the battery pack when storing for a long time.
