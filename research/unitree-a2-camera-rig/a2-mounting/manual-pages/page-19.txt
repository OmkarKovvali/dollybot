Switching off A2

Before shutting down, ensure the robot is standing on flat ground and is in a static standing state (the
robot's body position is at the initial state after powering on and standing up, with the body level, no joystick
input, and in a static standing posture).

1) Press the L2+A buttons twice. The robot will complete joint locking and lying down actions in
sequence.

2) After the robot enters the lying-down state, shut down one of the batteries by pressing the power button
briefly once, then pressing and holding the power button for more than 3 seconds.

After shutdown, arrange the robot’s upper legs, lower legs, and hip joint positions according to the body
placement requirements to prepare for the next startup. If the A2 will not be used for an extended period,

promptly remove the battery pack.
