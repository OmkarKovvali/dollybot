Start up A2

After the robot completes the pre-startup check and is positioned according to the pre-startup preparation
requirements, follow the steps below to power it on: First, briefly press the power switch once, then press and
hold the power switch for more than 3 seconds to turn on the battery. After the battery starts, wait for 2 minutes.

The robot will automatically stand up, with its body parallel to the ground, indicating a successful startup.

Q: @ When two batteries are inserted in the A2 battery compartment, it is only necessary to power on one of the
batteries via the button; there is no need to power on both batteries simultaneously using the buttons.
@ When powered by a single battery, the startup indicator light is green.
@ When both batteries are powered on, or when an additional battery is inserted into a robot operating on a
single battery, the indicator light will turn blue. In this state, only one battery is discharging, while the other is
in standby mode. When both batteries are discharging simultaneously, the indicator light will turn green. This
allows the machine to output maximum power. The condition for simultaneous discharge is that the voltages
and charge levels of the two batteries are essentially equal.
@ When both batteries are operating normally inside the battery compartment (with a blue or green light on),
removing one battery from the compartment will cause an immediate shutdown. Reinserting the battery will
cause an immediate startup. If a battery is manually powered off via its button, that battery will not be awakened
by the battery compartment for 15 seconds. It will require manual startup or must wait for more than 15 seconds

to be awakened by the battery compartment.

AN @ If the starting up fails, please check the placement of the body!
@ The upper leg being extended outward or the lower leg being pressed inward may cause the A2 to fail to start
up.
® Be careful of pinching hands at the motion joints. Exercise caution!
@ After the A2 starts up successfully, it will automatically stand up. Please maintain a safe distance!

@ When replacing the battery, the robot dog should remain stationary and not be subjected to external
disturbances (such as kicking the machine or adding loads to it, etc.). After the battery replacement is complete
(i.e., 3 seconds after the battery is fully inserted into the battery compartment), the robot can be used normally.

@ When the robot is operating in single-battery mode or in dual-battery blue-light standby mode, exercise
caution when performing vigorous movements (such as flips, rapid climbing, rapid descending, etc.), as such

operations can significantly shorten the battery's lifespan.
