Function Highlights

1) Strong Payload, Industry Expert

A2 possesses outstanding load-bearing capacity, with a continuous walking payload of 25kg and a
maximum static standing payload of 100kg. It can meet diverse load requirements in scenarios such as logistics

transportation and equipment handling, making it a reliable "strongman" for complex tasks.
2) Ultra-Long Endurance, Continuous Operation

A2 adopts a dual-battery system with a hot-swappable parallel architecture. Each battery has a capacity
of 9000mAh, providing a combined capacity of 18000mAh. The batteries utilize high-performance cells and
an advanced Battery Management System (BMS) independently developed by Unitree. The robot can walk
continuously for 5 hours/20km under no load and 3 hours/12km under full load. With seamless battery

swapping, missions remain uninterrupted, ensuring robust power support for long-duration operations.

3) Exceptional Power, Agile Mobility

A2 achieves a running speed of 5m/s, with a maximum step-climbing height of 30cm and a maximum
slope angle of 45°. It combines speed with obstacle-crossing capabilities, enabling rapid response to mission
demands and effortless handling of complex terrain challenges.

4) IP56-Level Protection, Reliable and Durable

A2 achieves an IP56 protection rating. Its body structure and key components are specially treated to

effectively resist dust ingress and high-pressure water jets. It can operate reliably in extreme temperatures

ranging from -20°C to 55°C, ensuring all-weather performance.

5) Open Platform, Flexible Expandability

A2 comes standard with an 8-core high-performance CPU (for platform functions) and an Intel Core 17
processor (for user development), with an optional high-computing module available. It provides multiple
expansion interfaces, including RS485 x2, CAN x2, Gigabit Ethernet x2, USB 3.0 Type-C x2, supporting the
integration of various peripherals. With its medium-sized form factor and powerful dynamic system, it
supports rapid development for multiple industry scenarios (such as logistics, inspection, and rescue), making

it a formidable choice for industrial-grade applications.
