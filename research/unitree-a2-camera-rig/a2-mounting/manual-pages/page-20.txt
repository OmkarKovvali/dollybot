Packing

Preparation for packing: Rotate the legs of the quadruped robot to the position shown in the picture (rear
leg retracted step: rotate the rear leg hip motors so that the rear thighs are placed in the position as the picture

shows, and at the same time retract the rear lower legs to the position shown in the picture.

OO

After completing the pre-packing preparations, first place the quadruped robot into the lower part of the
transportation case. After the quadruped robot is placed in the lower part of the case, put the accompanying
remote control, charger, etc. into their respective positions inside the transportation case to ensure that none

of the above items will fall out when the upper part of the case is closed.

Abnormal Condition Description

When using the A2 quadruped robot, the robot may be abnormal. Most abnormal situations are
controllable (there are solutions). The users should not panic when encountering these problems. Read the
following contents in detail and solve the problems step by step.

If you have any questions, please contact Unitree Robotics official technical support:
https://global-serviceconsole.unitree.com/#/work-order

1) Startup Self-Check Unsuccessful

If the robot lies on the ground during startup and does not stand up after waiting for 2 minutes, it indicates
that the startup self-check has failed, and the robot cannot stand up. In this case, it is necessary to recheck the
robot according to the "Pre-startup Check" and "Pre-startup Preparation" steps and try again.
