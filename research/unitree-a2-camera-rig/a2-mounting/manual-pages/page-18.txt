Additional Operating Instructions

a) Joystick Control: After the companion remote controller enters Joystick Mode, you can control the

robotic dog's movements via the joystick. While in joystick control mode, you may remove the remote

controller from the waist belt for operation. To resume Companion Mode, reattach the remote controller to the

waist belt before activating companion mode again.

b) In Companion Mode, the device achieves following functionality based on the relative position

between the remote controller and the robotic dog. Therefore, ensure the companion remote controller moves

along with the wearer's torso. Do not hold it and wave it around arbitrarily.

Companion Mode Precautions:

@ Do not use in narrow indoor spaces or areas with dense metal reflective surfaces.

@ Ensure that before entering or exiting Companion Mode, the button side faces to the right and the
antenna points vertically upward.

@ The maximum operating distance in Joystick Mode for the companion remote controller is
approximately 15 meters.

@ When Companion Mode is active, ensure the distance between the robotic dog and the remote
controller does not exceed 2.4 meters. Excessive distance may cause signal interruption, loss of
control, or other malfunctions.

@ When Companion Mode is active, do not run quickly. Maintain a steady walking speed of no more
than 2 m/s. Rapid movement can easily result in loss of remote control signals and loss of control
over the robotic dog, leading to potential safety hazards.

@ The optimal height difference between the companion remote controller and the robotic dog's head
should be maintained between 0.2m and 0.5m, as shown in Figure 2.

@ In Companion Mode, the optimal straight-line distance between the remote controller and the
robotic dog's head is approximately 0.8 meters.

@ In Companion Mode, the following function is achieved through the relative position of the remote
controller and the robotic dog. Therefore, ensure the companion remote controller moves along with
the wearer's torso. Do not hold it and wave it around arbitrarily.

@ When walking, try to proceed on flat, open ground without obstacles to prevent the robotic dog
from colliding with environmental obstacles during companion mode.

@ Do not use the Companion function on special terrains such as stairs or steep slopes. Such

environments are prone to rollovers, falls, and other issues that could damage the robotic dog.
