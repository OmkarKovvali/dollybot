A2 CAMERA RIG — INTERACTIVE PRINTED CONCEPT, REVISION 04

Run from the workspace root:
python3 -m http.server 8770 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory research/unitree-a2-camera-rig/viewer/dist
Open http://127.0.0.1:8770 in the browser or Codex panel.

Drag to rotate; scroll/pinch to zoom; right drag to pan.
Show robot toggles the actual A2 reference geometry. Robot opacity ranges
from fully transparent (0%) to solid (100%), defaulting to 65%.
Focus mount frames the camera assembly. Fit assembly includes the dog.
Inspect underside recess moves the view beneath the proposed pole screw.
Transparent cover helps inspect the recessed head. OEM reference switches
back to the unchanged original housing. The default Printed concept uses
the derived housing with actual cut geometry, not a painted-on hole.

OWNER'S CHANGE
All adapter structure is proposed as 3D printed: housing, reinforced stick
seat, camera platform, sliding quick-release and its locking pin. There
are no metal mounting plates, backing plates or washers in this revision.
Ordinary metal threaded fasteners are retained for the stick, camera and
factory rail nuts; the screws are not printed. The camera quick-release
is a new printed concept, not a Falcam product or certified Arca interface.
Its tolerances, capture, locking and strength have not been engineered yet.

ACTUAL REFERENCE GEOMETRY
- dist/assets/v1-cover.stl: unchanged official V1 STEP tessellation.
- dist/assets/a2-robot.stl: actual A2 from Unitree's assembled STEP,
  with the OEM dock removed and the remaining geometry registered to the
  standalone cover using four mounting-hole circles. No hand placement.
- dist/assets/robot-registration.json: source-to-viewer transform and
  residuals, independently checked against deck height and asymmetric
  housing extents. Revision differences exist between source dock solids.
- dist/assets/robot-mesh.json: mesh provenance, bounds and clearance notes.

DERIVED CONCEPT
- dist/assets/printed-cover-concept.stl: OEM-reference shape modified
  with a local printed reinforcement and real underside access well.
- dist/assets/printed-cover-concept.json: actual dimensions and checks.
- A provisional 7 mm smooth clearance bore takes a bottom-up metal 1/4-20
  screw. The 12 mm underside counterbore/access well opens to the outside
  and seats the screw head at Z=27 mm. The modeled head (10.5 mm diameter,
  6.5 mm high) lies from Z=20.5 to 27 mm, fully inside the housing envelope.
  These dimensions are provisional until an actual screw is selected.
  The well extends through the solid simplified housing to reach the
  underside; it is deeper than the head's height. No threaded geometry is
  printed in the bore, and no metal washer or reinforcement plate is used.
- The whole housing bottom is Z=0. Added structure does not extend below
  that plane. Fasten the pole from the underside before mounting the
  housing if the robot blocks tool access once assembled.

LIMITS
This is a review render with derived concept solids, not print-ready CAD.
The OEM model is simplified. Registration precision is not a manufacturing
fit tolerance. Static sampled clearance does not validate dynamic flex,
walking impacts, vibration, full leg motion, sensor coverage or tool access.
The robot still requires a physical rail/nut fit check and battery removal
check. Exact screw head, engagement, actual stick diameter and working
height remain to be measured. The Sony lens is an unidentified 18–50 mm;
its render is a shape placeholder. Camera bodies use published envelopes.
The stick-height slider is illustrative and is not a safe extension rating.

Three.js, OrbitControls and STLLoader are vendored under THREE-LICENSE.txt.
All viewer assets are local; no external network is needed.

OBSERVED STATIC FIT CHECK
Exact OpenCASCADE solid intersections found 0 mm^3 overlap with the main
robot body for the July assembled dock, the May standalone cover, and the
derived printed cover. See dist/assets/cover-body-intersection.json.
At the pole axis (78,0), the exact robot roof is Z=-0.38108 mm; the original
cover underside is Z=3.44987 mm, giving 3.83095 mm axial gap. The proposed
head's bottom Z=20.5 mm sits 20.88108 mm above that robot roof along the
axis. These are local measurements in simplified CAD, not minimum global
clearances or fabrication tolerances. The body's higher points elsewhere
in the pole footprint do not intersect the actual cover surface.
Install the stick screw with the cover removed, then attach the cover to
the rails; access for a driver from below after installation is not proven.
The whole-assembly exclusion check subsequently confirmed that body10 is
the only one of 47 robot volumes with a bounding box overlapping the
entire padded cover-and-boss envelope. All other 46 volumes are disjoint.
Thus the zero exact common volume covers this printed housing against all
47 static robot solids, not only a sampled point under the pole.
