    Description

    【Precise CNC Technology】Nearly all metal made with CNC and anodic oxidation. It is made of sturdy aluminum alloy and stainless steel.
    【Low Profile Gravity】Low center of gravity design makes its gravity center 10mm lower than normal ball heads. There is a rubber-padded mount on the top to protect your camera.
    【Multi-Angles】It can achieve 360 ° horizontal rotation, -30 ° to+30 ° tilt, and is designed with double U-shaped notches.
    【Portable & Wide Application】Net weight is only 0.13lb/59g, height is 1.87in/47.5mm, max load is 6.61 lb/3kg. 1/4 top screw and 3/8 bottom screw hole allow you to use with various equipments, such as camera, phone holder, fill light, tripod and monopod.

    Questions & Answers

    How should I choose the height of my tripod?

    ·The general rule for selecting the appropriate height is that, when the tripod is fully extended (with the center column either not raised or raised only partially), the combined height of the tripod, head, and camera should be slightly lower than or equal to the height of your eyes when looking straight through the viewfinder.
    ·Reference Height: Optimal tripod height = maximum height without extending the center column ≈ your eye level - head height - camera body height.
    ·If you have additional requirements, you can select a tripod based on your specific needs.
    Can tripods and heads from different brands be mixed and matched?

    Yes, but please note the following three key points:
    Thread type and size: Most tripods use either 3/8'‘-16 (common in professional tripods) or 1/4’'-20 (common in consumer-grade products). There are many high-quality adapter rings or connectors available on the market to adapt between these two thread specifications.
