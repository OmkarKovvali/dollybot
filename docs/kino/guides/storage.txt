Search and filter your library

Type in the search field at the top of the media panel to find clips by what’s said in them or what’s on screen. Each clip marks where the match is on its strip, and Add to timeline puts just those moments on your timeline. Choose Says or Shows to search only the transcript or only the picture, and add a person to find the clips they’re in. ⌘K searches transcripts, people and bins across your whole organization.

Click Filter to narrow the library by type, duration, codec, camera, lens, frame rate, shoot day and more. Each value shows how many clips have it. Save a filter and it becomes a smart bin in your bin tree, which fills in as new footage arrives.
