Footage and storage

Kino transcribes every clip, describes each shot and recognizes the people in it.

Kino keeps a 720p proxy of every clip in the cloud by default, so your team can play it on any computer. Originals stay on your drive until you upload them.

Pro media formats

These formats play in Kino without transcoding.

Apple ProRes
422 Proxy, 422 LT, 422, 422 HQ, 4444 and 4444 XQ, with alpha in the 4444 family
Avid DNxHD and DNxHR
All common profiles, including OP-Atom
Blackmagic RAW
All compression settings, constant bitrate and constant quality
REDCODE RAW
V-RAPTOR, KOMODO and the RED2 generation. Older RED sensors aren’t supported
H.264 and HEVC
Including inside MXF
Motion JPEG, DV, DivX and Xvid
Legacy camera and archive footage in AVI, including DivX 3

Exports render to H.264, HEVC, VP9, AV1 and ProRes. ARRI RAW and Canon Cinema RAW Light aren’t supported.

Search and filter your library

Type in the search field at the top of the media panel to find clips by what’s said in them or what’s on screen. Each clip marks where the match is on its strip, and Add to timeline puts just those moments on your timeline. Choose Says or Shows to search only the transcript or only the picture, and add a person to find the clips they’re in. ⌘K searches transcripts, people and bins across your whole organization.

Click Filter to narrow the library by type, duration, codec, camera, lens, frame rate, shoot day and more. Each value shows how many clips have it. Save a filter and it becomes a smart bin in your bin tree, which fills in as new footage arrives.

Storage per plan

Proxies, uploaded originals and imports from Dropbox, Google Drive or Shade count toward storage; originals on your own drives don’t. See the plan comparison for allowances.
